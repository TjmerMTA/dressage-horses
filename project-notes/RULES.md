# The client's standing rules for this project

Notebook v1 — updated 2026-11-04

1. Do not ask the client yes/no or which-option questions. Choose the most sensible
   option, say in one line what was assumed, and finish the work. (Global rule.)
2. Answer the client in Russian/Ukrainian — the language they wrote in. The team
   works in English internally.
3. Keep the design of the client's mock-up. Visual changes are shown, not decided.
4. Do not delete or break the old `pflegedienst-alno` repository.
