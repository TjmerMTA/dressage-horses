# About this project

Notebook v1 — updated 2026-11-04

A website for a Belgian company with 6+ years of experience that finds **dressage
horses** in Belgium, Germany and the Netherlands for buyers all over the world:
search, tryout, vet check, paperwork and delivery to the buyer's stable.

- Audience: international dressage riders and their trainers. Language: English.
- The business model is lead generation — a 4-question quiz, then WhatsApp contact.
- Built from a single-file HTML mock-up the client made, plus 32 professional photos.
- First home: https://dev.siteboosty.com (a demo address, so it can be shown to people).
- Not yet confirmed by the client: the company's real name, logo, WhatsApp number,
  privacy/imprint texts, and whether the horses in the catalogue are real listings or
  samples. The site is being built so all of these are editable from the admin panel.
