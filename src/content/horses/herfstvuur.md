A chestnut mare with a white blaze, a lot of presence and a very kind head. She
competes at S level and is confirmed in the collected work: half-passes, flying
changes in sequence, and the first steps of piaffe are coming.

Her trot is her best gait — light, uphill and regular, as you can see in the photos.
She is easy to hack out on her own or in company and is calm in the stable.

Suits an ambitious amateur or a young rider who wants to move towards small tour.
