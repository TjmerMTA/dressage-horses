This policy explains which personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it and what your rights are. We are a company established in Belgium, so the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Belgian data protection law apply.

## Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is the company named in the [imprint](/imprint/). You can reach us for any privacy question on WhatsApp or through the contact details listed there.

## What we collect

**When you just visit the website.** The website is a static site. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Like every web server, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc.) processes your IP address and basic technical information about your request in order to deliver the page and keep the service secure.

**When you answer the quiz.** If you complete the 4 quick questions and send the form, we receive:

- your answers: where you are from, your budget and how you want to buy;
- your name and your WhatsApp number;
- the address of the page you sent the form from, the time you sent it and, if present in the link you followed, campaign parameters such as `utm_source`.

## Why we use it

We use your quiz answers and contact details only to contact you on WhatsApp about your request and to prepare your personal selection of horses. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give with the checkbox in the form, and the steps you ask us to take before a possible purchase (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

We do not send you marketing spam, and we do not sell or share your details with third parties for their own purposes.

## Who receives it

Your data is processed by service providers that help us run the website and receive your request (website hosting and the service that stores the quiz requests). They act on our instructions only. When we contact you on WhatsApp, WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) processes the messages under its own terms. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; where that happens, it is covered by the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep it

We keep your request for as long as we are working on it with you and, if no purchase follows, for no longer than 12 months after our last contact. If a purchase follows, we keep what the law requires us to keep for accounting and tax purposes.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. To use any of these rights, write to us on WhatsApp or through the contact details in the imprint.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Belgium this is the Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), [dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be).

## Changes

We update this policy when the way we handle data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
